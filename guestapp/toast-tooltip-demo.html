What changed and why

Each improvement maps to a concrete gap in the shipped app or a divergence inside this prototype set.

Surface

One toast, not four

Now: the prototypes grew four one-off toasts (dm-toast, pay-toast, es-toast, bx-toast) with different positions and paddings, and the shipped app ships sonner’s stock richColors — flat green/red slabs that match nothing else in the design.

Proposed: one component. Dark-glass surface (toasts are chrome, so glass is allowed over content), status carried only by the icon accent and timer color. Every flow fires the same thing.

Placement

Placement is a rule, not a choice

Now: position="bottom-right" everywhere. On a phone that corner is under the thumb, behind the CTA dock and the bottom nav — exactly where a toast gets covered or mis-tapped.

Proposed: mobile pins under the glass top bar, desktop stays bottom-right. Callers can’t choose — the CTA dock and bottom nav are never covered, and a sideways swipe dismisses on touch.

Timing

Time you can see — and pause

Now: one invisible timer for everything: duration={5000}, errors included. A toast can vanish mid-read, and an error you looked away from is simply gone.

Proposed: a hairline timer shows what’s left; hovering (or resting a finger) pauses it. Success 4 s, warning 6 s — and errors never auto-dismiss.

Trust

Errors speak human

Now: toastResponseError(error) pipes the raw API message to the guest — machine phrasing, sometimes untranslated, with no way forward.

Proposed: what happened, in guest words + “nothing was lost” + a Retry action + a short Ref code support can actually use. The raw message goes to Sentry, not the toast.

Discipline

Undo beats confirm; the stack stays quiet

Now: up to five toasts stack (visibleToasts={5}), duplicates included, and there’s no action slot — so light removals either interrupt with a confirm or offer no way back.

Proposed: act first with a 5 s Undo; cap the stack at 3 with Clear all; a re-fired toast id pulses the existing toast instead of stacking a twin.

Access

Tooltips that work without a mouse

Now: hover-only tooltips are dead weight on touch — the info just doesn’t exist on a phone, and truncated names stay unreadable.

Proposed: desktop tooltips show on keyboard focus too; touch gets tap popovers (short) and bottom sheets (long); truncated values reveal on tap. Toasts announce as role="status" / role="alert", and reduced-motion swaps animation for plain timeouts.
