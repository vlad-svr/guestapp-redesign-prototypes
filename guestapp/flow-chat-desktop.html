Chat with host — Interactive Flow (Desktop)

On desktop the thread becomes a proper destination: the conversation sits in the middle, and a right context rail carries the property card (photo, role, response time), the booking summary both sides share, the shared-files gallery and common questions — so the guest never has to re-explain their stay. Covers the active conversation (including per-guest sender colors when several guests share the booking), photos, video and files with pending uploads and a deleted-file placeholder, the delivery / read receipts lifecycle with retry, the first-message state, the identity check and the chat-off state.
