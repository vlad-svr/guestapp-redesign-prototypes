Splash screen — the arrival moment (Desktop · SDK · Kiosk)

The same handoff at three sizes. On a laptop the splash dissolves into the navy-sidebar shell, and the mark lands on the sidebar's brand tile. Embedded in a client's website via the SDK it is scoped to the widget box — it has no business painting over the host's own page. On the kiosk it is read from a metre away, and it has nowhere to fly to, so it simply settles. Mock data only.

The error & recovery states sit in this flow too — the arrival is where they happen, and the 20 s watchdog above lands on one of them. On desktop they are a centred glass card on the branded gradient: errors happen pre-auth, before any app shell exists, so they reuse the Find-booking pre-auth shell rather than the sidebar.
